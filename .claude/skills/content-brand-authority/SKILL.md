## Video Strategy (Reels/TikTok/Shorts)

### Format Rules
- Vertical (9:16) for Reels/TikTok, landscape for YouTube/MLS
- 15-30 seconds for Reels, 60-90 seconds for YouTube Shorts
- Hook in first 2 seconds — or they scroll
- Captions/on-screen text required (80% watch with sound off)
- DM shares = #1 Reels ranking signal (5-10x reach boost)
- 3-second hold rate > 60% = 5-10x more reach

### Video Content Categories (rotate weekly)

**Category 1: Market Intel (builds authority)**
- "Vegas homes sold X% faster this week — here's why"
- "This neighborhood just hit [milestone] — what it means for you"
- Price updates, inventory shifts, rate changes

**Category 2: Neighborhood Tours (builds local expertise)**
- "3 things nobody tells you about [community]"
- "Best kept secret in [area] — [specific amenity/feature]"
- Walk-and-talk format, show real locations

**Category 3: Behind the Scenes (builds trust)**
- "What really happens at a home inspection"
- "I just negotiated $30K off for my buyer — here's how"
- Day-in-the-life, showing prep, contract moments

**Category 4: Tips & Education (builds value)**
- "First-time buyer? Here's your 3-step Vegas plan"
- "Selling? The #1 mistake that costs you $20K"
- Buyer/seller advice, seasonal tips, common questions

**Category 5: Community/Lifestyle (builds connection)**
- "My favorite restaurant in [neighborhood]"
- "Free things to do in Vegas this weekend"
- Events, openings, local business spotlights

